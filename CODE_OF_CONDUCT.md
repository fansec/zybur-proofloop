# Code of conduct

Zybur Proofloop is maintained by Zybur and built with anyone who wants to measure detections honestly.
Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space) is
expected to:

- be respectful and patient, and assume good intent;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- accept that a measurement can prove your change or your rule wrong, and say so when it proves
  someone else's wrong, kindly;
- keep real attack payloads, credentials and data from anyone's environment out of the project.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery,
sexual attention, threats, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers at security@zybur.nl. Reports are read by the
maintainers only and kept confidential. The maintainers may remove comments, commits or other
contributions that break this code, and may block anyone who breaks it repeatedly or seriously.
They answer every report and say what they did.

This code applies in every project space and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
